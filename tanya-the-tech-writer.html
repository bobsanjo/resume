05 · about

Written by people who've shipped the thing.

"Good documentation isn't a writing problem. It's an understanding problem — you have to know the system before you can explain it."

Tanya started as a software engineer before moving into technical writing, and built her team the same way: writers who can read the code, sit in on standups, and ask the right questions before drafting a word. Most of the team holds an engineering or science degree, and that background shapes how they work — fewer assumptions, more questions in the first call, and drafts your engineers don't have to rewrite.
